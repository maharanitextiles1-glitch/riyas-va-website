About

Leadership beyond titles

A journey shaped by vision, discipline and people.

Riyas V. A. leads the Atlas–Maharani Group with a customer-first approach and a strong focus on building sustainable businesses, meaningful employment and memorable retail experiences.

His leadership style combines the discipline of modern retail with a deep understanding of Kerala’s fashion, wedding and family-shopping market.

Beyond business growth, his approach places equal importance on team culture, customer trust, employee recognition and community-oriented initiatives.

Personal foundation

Family, values & perspective.

A more personal side of the journey, expressed with discretion while keeping private family information away from the public profile.

Beyond business

Strong businesses often begin with strong personal foundations.

For Riyas V. A., family represents balance, perspective and continuity. Away from the pace of business, it is the space that keeps priorities clear, decisions grounded and success connected to responsibility.

The same values reflected in his leadership — respect, commitment, consistency and care for people — are also central to the way he approaches life beyond work.
